Guide

Goal

Navigate the maze to find the exit. Use arrow keys or swipe gestures to move. Collect keys by completing levels to track your progress.

Special Abilities

In special modes (Time, Dark, Enemy, Ghost, Zone), you get a unique ability button. Instead of costing currency, these have usage limits based on difficulty:

Easy 1 Use
Medium 2 Uses
Hard 3 Uses

Modes & Monsters

Time Mode

Race against the clock. Use the ability button to add bonus time.

Dark Mode

Visibility is limited. Use the ability button to temporarily illuminate the maze.

Enemy Mode

Avoid guardians. Use ability to freeze them for 5s.

  • Blue: Balanced stats.
  • Yellow: Fast speed, low vision.
  • Purple: Very fast, high vision.

Ghost Mode

Invisible stalkers. They appear when close. Use ability to reveal them for 5s.

Zone Mode

A deadly flood consumes the maze. Reach the Safe Zone (Green) and wait until the flood passes. Use Shield ability to dissipate a wave instantly.
